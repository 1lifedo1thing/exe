- On Windows, `exe ssh` reaches a VM again on a PC where Codex has run: the VM key is kept to its user.
- **Mac OS X** has an **Agent** button: a Chat that works inside Tiger and uploads Workspace files.
- Claude Code window: **Get Info** on a session; its latest prompt over the terminal, a click lists them all.
- On a phone, a picture window zooms: pinch the picture or double-tap it, and drag it about while zoomed.
- **Extras…** on Welcome's Apps card has a fourth step, **Projects**, an issue tracker beside exe. In it, **Open in Claude Code** on a project's menu starts a session in its folder, and **Left** and **Right**, or a sideways swipe on a phone, step through an issue's pictures.
- **Stickies**: press the pushpin at a note's foot in Notes and the note stands on the desk as a small window in its colour, where you left it, on every desk that syncs. Its close box takes it off again.
- **Show Desktop**, left of Show All Windows in the menu bar: open windows slide off to the edges and back.
- `exe expose host -dir /www/site`: a folder served as a site by exe itself; a `.password` file guards it.
